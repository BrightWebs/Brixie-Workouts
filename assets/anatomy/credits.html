The full-body male model is the mobile adaptation by Fit Mit With of openly licensed anatomy. The downloadable source model is redistributed unchanged. Rebuild changes its display with neutral materials, pink-red exercise-group highlights, paired front/back camera views, surface-layer filtering and isolated-target views. Facial anatomy is clipped out and replaced with an original smooth, featureless head. The displayed adaptations are available under CC BY-SA 4.0.
